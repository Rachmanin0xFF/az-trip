Leopold,

We both knew there was no point. The entire place had been scouted already, mapped out in its entirety. When I hauled myself out of the last fruitless dwelling, I remember seeing you staring out at the night sky and laughing. I had never seen a man more detached than you, Leo. Ground yourself here. A king is nothing without his pawns; a castle tumbles without its supporters.
