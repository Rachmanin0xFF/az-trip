The crime? A wealthy asshole named Arthur T. Budgerington is alleging that four men, all in disguise, have stolen four of his most precious artifacts. The Pitt Krew members have been detained by law enforcement and brought to Silly Jail, where they face intense scrutiny from Detective Sophia and her partner, Slice.

The facts are jumbled, but deductive reasoning can be used to get to the bottom of this mess!

Here's what we know: a fancy gold quilt, rare Salvador Dali painting, collection of faberge eggs, and a statue of Poseidon's trident have been stolen by four people -- each taking one object. But who took what and smuggled it out wearing which?

The facts are these:
